# Vision

Build a professional investment platform capable of:

- Collecting verified market data
- Calculating technical indicators
- Evaluating fundamentals
- Monitoring news
- Scoring investment opportunities
- Assisting portfolio management
- Supporting long-term investing and position trading

---

# Development Philosophy

Every version must be:

- Stable
- Tested
- Documented
- Reproducible

No unfinished features are released.
